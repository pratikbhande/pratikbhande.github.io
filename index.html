// About Me

Engineer. Builder. Writer.

Designing AI systems that survive contact with production.

I'm Pratik Bhande, an AI and GenAI/ML Engineer at QED42, where I build and operate production-scale AI systems used in real enterprise environments. I own the full lifecycle — from model and retrieval design to deployment, monitoring, and continuous optimisation.

I'm the core engineer behind Aeldris, a live, production-deployed enterprise AI platform powering semantic search, conversational AI, and knowledge retrieval across large and sensitive datasets. My work spans multimodal ingestion pipelines, vector database design, embedding optimisation, enterprise-grade AI safety with guardrails, PII detection, and content filtering.

I build high-performance AI services using FastAPI, observability-first systems with OpenTelemetry and SigNoz, and analytics pipelines using ClickHouse. Beyond production work, I explore advanced architectures including adaptive RAG with reinforcement learning, semantic video RAG, and multi-agent orchestration systems.

I actively write technical articles, speak at conferences, and contribute to the AI engineering community. I believe the best AI engineers don't just build models — they build systems that work reliably at scale.
